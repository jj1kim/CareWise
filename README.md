# CareWise
---
## About Project
- Product for young adults, who are unfamiliar with clothing management.
- This provides full-management about carelabel with fine-tuned LLM.
  - Carelabel recognition, response with professionalism, wiki of carelabel, etc.
- Especially focused on a natural and specific response of LLM, and easy usage of our product
